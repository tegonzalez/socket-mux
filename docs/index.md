# socket-mux Authority Index

## Purpose

Route readers and authors of socket-mux to the owners of its goals, constraints, roadmap, specification, design and known issues without restating them, and route every open question through those owners before it reaches the project owner. The declared scope is the whole socket-mux tool: both the TX and RX modes. Start with the [goals](goals.md).

The numbers are the first-fill seeds for constraints (95), goals (90), roadmap (75), specification (60) and design (45): the constraints and goals are the shortest and most human-facing statements of what must not happen and what must hold, the specification states consumer-visible behavior that realizes them, and the design states mechanism beneath both. The known issues register (70) has no first-fill seed in DocGraph, and the register records residuals against the specification without competing with it. The roadmap's claims carry prospective applicability, so they do not compete with the current-baseline claims of the specification or the design. The constraints govern implementation patterns, not consumer-visible behavior, so they do not compete with the specification.

## Query Routes

| Question                                              | Kind    | Ordered owners                                                             | Output                                                                                                                                         | Missing-input behavior                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What must socket-mux achieve?                         | direct  | goals                                                                      | Outcomes                                                                                                                                       | Report the missing goals owner                                                                                                                                                    |
| What does TX or RX accept, emit and guarantee?        | derived | goals -> specification                                                     | Public surface, invariants and gaps                                                                                                            | Gate on the first missing owner                                                                                                                                                   |
| How are TX and RX built, and why?                     | derived | goals -> specification -> design                                           | Architecture, decisions and rationale                                                                                                          | Gate on the first missing owner                                                                                                                                                   |
| Which implementation patterns are forbidden?          | direct  | constraints                                                                | Forbidden patterns with their reasons and detection signals                                                                                    | Report that no constraint exists                                                                                                                                                  |
| Which prospects does the roadmap hold?                | derived | goals -> roadmap                                                           | Prospects with their disposition, prospective requirements and acceptance criteria                                                             | Report that no prospect exists                                                                                                                                                    |
| How is the socket-mux CLI invoked?                    | derived | goals -> specification                                                     | Grammar, options, precedence and exit statuses                                                                                                 | Gate on the first missing owner                                                                                                                                                   |
| Which residuals in current behavior are known?        | derived | goals -> specification -> known issues                                     | Admitted residuals with their owner, disposition and retirement condition                                                                      | Report that no known issue exists                                                                                                                                                 |
| How is an open question or pending decision answered? | derived | goals -> constraints -> specification -> design -> known issues -> roadmap | The answer from the first owner that settles it, cited at that owner; otherwise the roadmap `Proposal` prospect that holds it with its options | Add the unanswered question to a `Proposal` prospect in the roadmap, then ask the project owner that one question with its context, two to four options and a recommended default |

An open question is answered from these owners before anyone asks the project owner. Many questions are settled by a goal, a constraint, a specified behavior or a design decision; an answer found there is applied and cited, not asked again. Only a question that no owner settles is escalated, and it is held as a `Proposal` prospect in the roadmap until it is decided. A decision, once given, is written into the owner that governs its subject: behavior into the specification, mechanism into the design, a forbidden pattern into the constraints, an accepted residual into the known issues; its `Proposal` prospect then retires with a successor reference. A requester decision awaiting that capture may be held verbatim in a decisions intake register, which carries no authority, is not an owner in this index, and drains into these owners.

## Durable Boundary

The authority basis consists only of the listed owners. Task assignment and work progress have no owner in this index.

Background research (bandwidth techniques, prior art and source excerpts) is in [research](research/); it is reference material and carries no authority.
